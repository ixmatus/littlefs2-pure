# ADR-NNNN: short title

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | superseded
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision now. Who or what is downstream. Why the obvious alternative is insufficient.

## Decision

What is being adopted. State it as a single declarative sentence first, then expand.

## Consequences

**Wins.** The benefits the decision produces.

**Costs.** The price paid. Be honest; future maintainers need to know what they are inheriting.

**Explicitly out of scope.** What this decision does not cover, so a future reader does not infer a guarantee that was never made.

## Related

Pointers to commits, plans, prior ADRs, and external sources.
